# Dimension Five - Interior Design 5D Planner

`The Problem: Designing a room or managing a renovation project is a juggling act. You spend hours planning layouts, picking materials, setting deadlines, and tracking expenses, often in separate tools. This leads to costly surprises, missed deadlines, and major stress when the design vision doesn't match the budget reality.`

## The Product Story - What is Dimension Five?

Planner 5D is an all-in-one platform for interior design and renovation project management. It unites 3D modeling, project timelines, and real-time budgeting so you can see your design, schedule the work, and instantly know the cost impact of every choice you make.

> Imagine: As you drag a new sofa into your digital room model, the app instantly updates your total project cost and the required delivery schedule. No more guessing games!

## Why It Matters

We empower users to maintain complete financial and visual control over their design projects. By linking the five dimensions, Space, Item, Time, and Cost, we eliminate budget overruns and scheduling conflicts. This allows homeowners and designers to focus on creativity, not just spreadsheets.

`Our Goal: To make high-quality, complex design and renovation projects transparent, predictable, and stress-free.`

## Who Is This For?

This product is essential for anyone involved in or loves the feel of home design and renovation:

1. Homeowners managing their own DIY projects or coordinating major remodels.

2. Independent Interior Designers looking for a tool to share interactive designs and budgets seamlessly with clients.

3. Contractors and Project Managers who need to link the build schedule directly to material costs.

## Key Features (The Integrated 5D System)

1. 3D Spatial Design: Intuitive drag-and-drop tool for creating and visualizing room layouts.
Product Catalog Integration: Easily import furniture and material specs/costs from major retailers.

2. Real-Time 5D Budgeting: Every item added to the 3D model automatically updates the project budget summary.

3. 4D Scheduling & Timeline: Link installation tasks directly to material delivery dates and contractor availability. Drag-and-drop calendar view with automatic dependency linking.
   
4. Forecasting & Alerts: Get constant reminders to go over a budget or check a missed deadline.

5. Collaboration Hub: Share your live 3D model, budget, and timeline with clients or contractors in one link.
   
6. Smart Template Library: Reusable templates for common projects (e.g., "Office Layout," "Two-Storey House”).
